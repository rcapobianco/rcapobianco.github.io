My name is Rogério Capobianco, and I hold a PhD in Physics from the University of São Paulo. My main focus is in the realm of theoretical physics, with interests in gravity, dynamical systems, integrability, and mathematical modeling.

This page is part of the project **Phase Space Echoes**, where we revisit fundamental questions in physics, sometimes from a didactic perspective, and sometimes from a more technical or research-oriented point of view.
